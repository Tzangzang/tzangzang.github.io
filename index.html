Review Gaming 24h

A private publishing tool for the YouTube channel Review Gaming 24h.

What it does

Review Gaming 24h runs on the channel owner's own computer. It edits gaming highlight clips from streamers who allow clipping, adds English subtitles, and uploads the finished Shorts to the Review Gaming 24h channel using the YouTube Data API, with titles, descriptions and scheduled publish times.

It is used only by the channel owner. It does not offer sign-in to the public and does not access anyone else's YouTube account.
